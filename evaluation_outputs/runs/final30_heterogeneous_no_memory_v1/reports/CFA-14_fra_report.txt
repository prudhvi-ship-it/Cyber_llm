- Audit and rotate any secrets/keys stored in etcd that may have been accessed; review and revoke leases created during the incident.
- Enable and monitor etcd logs; add detections for unexpected /v3/kv and /v3/watch access from untrusted sources.
- Harden configuration management/IaC to enforce secure defaults (auth enabled, TLS required, restricted listen addresses) and include regular exposure scans.
