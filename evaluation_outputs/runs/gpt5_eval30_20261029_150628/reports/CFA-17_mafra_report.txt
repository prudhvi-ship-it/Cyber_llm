Defensive Recommendation:
- Restrict exposure of TCP/61616 to trusted networks using firewall/ACLs; prefer binding the broker to internal interfaces.
- Enable TLS for OpenWire and require authentication/authorization (e.g., JAAS); disable anonymous or unauthenticated access.
- Upgrade Apache ActiveMQ to a currently supported, patched release and follow vendor security advisories; apply mitigations for known issues referenced by CISA KEV.
- Monitor broker/system logs for anomalous connection patterns and configuration abuse (e.g., unexpected vm:// transport or brokerConfig=xbean:http:// usage); alert on repeated banner-only probes.
- Deploy network monitoring/IDS around broker ports (e.g., 61616, 5672) and retain PCAPs to distinguish reconnaissance from exploitation attempts.
- Reduce unnecessary service exposure and consider rate-limiting or tarpitting repeated unauthenticated connection attempts.
