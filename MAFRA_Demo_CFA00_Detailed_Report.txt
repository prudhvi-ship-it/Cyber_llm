- Monitor and alert on EPMD connections and Erlang rex RPC usage; log and review CouchDB/Erlang node events for os:cmd or unexpected process spawns.

- Rotate credentials, secrets, and Erlang cookies post-incident; invalidate any previously exposed values across the cluster.
